# Supported human intervention estimand

Status: design draft. Assignment verification, overlap audit and confirmatory power remain open. This document does not establish a human transfer result.

## Observable target and assignment

KuaiRand's official description records random replacement of recommended items from a candidate pool: https://github.com/chongminggao/KuaiRand . Its `is_rand` flag identifies intervened exposures. The observed overall fraction of random records is not a known individual propensity. Do not substitute that fraction into an inverse-propensity estimator without evidence of the assignment mechanism and eligible population. The 15 tab/scenario policies also require separate support checks.

The initial supported outcome is the recorded click/valid-play response to an individual exposed item. Analyses stratify by documented scenario and retain user-clustered sampling. Concurrent exposures share the same pre-timestamp history. Full-period item popularity, realized watch duration and post-assignment feedback never become pretreatment predictors.

A candidate intervention family changes the distribution over item groups in the documented randomized pool. Groups must use stable item metadata known at exposure time, such as verified video-duration bins, or a grouping frozen from training-only item information. The current `duration_ms` log column cannot automatically establish feature availability. Verify its agreement with stable item metadata, upload dates and the assignment pool before using it as context.

## Identification boundary

For randomized items, evaluate observed outcomes under supported group exposure conditions on independent users/times. If uniform selection from a fixed pool is established, calculate a group's assignment probability from the actual eligible pool membership, with separate scenario/time pools if necessary. Check consistency, missing outcomes, treatment support, contemporaneous exposure dependence and whether pool membership changes. If uniform selection or eligibility is not documented adequately, report a conditional observed-response prediction comparison and avoid a causal effect claim.

Do not treat randomized item exposure as randomization of arbitrary checkout designs, interfaces, fees or entire future recommendation trajectories. A one-exposure intervention evaluation supports a one-step response claim. Multi-step counterfactual trajectory evaluation requires sequential assignment and overlap assumptions or a separate authorized product experiment. Record this as a separate scope limitation even if one-step transfer succeeds.

## Frozen evaluation design

Train behavioral encoders on the designated training observations. Learn initial abstractions under training exposure environments. Define candidate environment groups using training data, fix search budgets and baselines, and select witnesses using only the search/validation observations. Compare a proposed state-pair's disagreement on prespecified outcomes, not arbitrary post hoc psychological interpretations.

Refine the partition using the same additional observations available to controls. Include random splits, extra states without targeted splits, extra data without refinement, random environment selection, fixed grids, a simple active-design baseline, and strong continuous sequence predictors. Match sample budgets, state capacity and tuning effort.

Freeze the chosen groups, target, horizon, weighting, model fits and tests before opening final intervention observations. Evaluate on held-out randomized observations with user-level clustering. Keep discovery data separate from certification calibration; use fresh calibration or a guarantee that covers adaptive selection. Never repeatedly tune on final intervention outcomes.

Primary result: paired user-weighted change in proper prediction loss on independent supported intervention responses, with confidence intervals and a multiplicity policy. Report group counts, user counts, effective sample sizes, overlap, width of intervals, and rare-state exclusions. A state pair that looks different in a learned simulator is a candidate witness; real outcomes must establish whether the distinction improves human prediction.

## Assignment evidence checked on 2026-10-15

Section 3.2 of the [dataset paper](https://arxiv.org/html/2208.08696) describes list-level insertion with a fixed but undisclosed probability, replacement of a randomly selected list position, and uniform item choice from 7,583 videos. It also states that the released users were retained only after at least ten random exposures. The release therefore selects users using their realized full-period exposure history.

Conditional item probability in the documented source mechanism is 1/7,583. It is different from the unknown probability of selecting an impression for replacement. Neither the published overall replacement fraction nor a fitted flag classifier supplies the withheld list-level probability. Release selection can further affect ignorability, especially if earlier interventions change later participation. A causal off-policy claim requires an explicit argument addressing that selection, list context and sequential eligibility.

The currently feasible human experiment evaluates predictions on observed random-exposure responses in the released cohort, with separate item-group and scenario support checks. Its scope is conditional observed-response prediction under the documented intervention data. It does not establish population treatment effects, arbitrary interface transfer, or counterfactual multi-step trajectories. Current-item identity is available as exposure context; month-aggregated item response statistics are excluded as future-derived features.

## Measured pool context audit

The full random-source item IDs reconcile to 7,583. The checksum-bound basic metadata has every reindexed item ID. Among pool items, usable positive durations are available for 7,344 items and unavailable for 239. Four prespecified duration groups, at most 7 seconds, 7 to 18 seconds, 18 to 60 seconds, and over 60 seconds, contain 42, 997, 1,775 and 4,530 items respectively. At 1,149,139 random-source rows with both durations usable, logged and basic-metadata durations match exactly. These are context-consistency checks, not outcome or causality evidence.

Any eventual known-duration-pool analysis must state its restriction explicitly and preserve the unknown-duration group in the support report. Conditional uniform source item assignment does not resolve release-cohort selection or identify the withheld list-insertion probability. Standard-case metadata agreement and final intervention case/power audits remain open. See `reports/preflight/kuairand-27k-item-metadata-audit.json`.

## Candidate implementation design before any final intervention scores

The current D6 quantizer clusters a representation containing the exposed item and duration. It must not be reused as a pre-exposure state for asking how the same history responds to different item groups. The existing frozen GRU also exposes `encode(tokens)`, which depends only on available prior feedback. The proposed human refinement extension uses this history representation, with recorded scenario as a conditioning stratum, and keeps the original D6 primary analysis unchanged.

Build a separate cohort retaining all `is_rand == 1` exposures in the frozen calendar and user roles. Multiple exposures at one timestamp share the same available preceding history; do not collapse them to the multistep representative. Preserve immutable physical case IDs. `randomized_cohort.py` implements response-free cohort selection and duration groups. Its scenario/duration identifiers are predictive context labels. Scenario is held fixed when comparing item-group witnesses; it is not itself a randomized intervention.

Four known-duration groups use the previously declared boundaries of 7, 18 and 60 seconds. The fifth group retains unknown or unavailable intrinsic duration for support reporting. It is not an admissible intervention for active search. Item-group membership uses checksum-bound intrinsic metadata with the same prior-upload-date availability restriction. The empirical random fraction is never used as an impression propensity.

Fit a history-state quantizer using training features only. Construct the initial partition from standard-policy observations. Counterexample discovery uses a separate calibration subset of randomized responses and searches the finite supported scenario/item-group family for state-pair outcome disagreement. A candidate is a model witness, not established human divergence. Estimate final group-conditioned point-model emissions on a disjoint calibration subset, give all methods the same observations, and report numerical quotient errors against that final fitted model. This separates adaptive witness selection from the data used for the final point kernel; it does not create an iid human confidence guarantee.

Controls must include no refinement with the same additional data, random environment selection, fixed environment grids, a simple uncertainty-based selection baseline, random capacity-matched splitting, and the original continuous predictors. Report query counts and exposure counts separately: searching a pre-existing table is a model-query experiment, not active acquisition of new human responses. Freeze the exact history quantizer, calibration partition rule, search/refinement budgets, supported-cell thresholds, loss aggregation, inference procedure and all input/model hashes before scoring final random-response observations.

Evaluate one-step observed responses on held-out times and users as appropriate to each protocol. Primary human-refinement comparisons use paired proper loss with user clustering and a declared multiplicity policy. Group support uses at least 50 users and 2,000 cases for inferential cells, with unsupported and unknown-duration strata retained descriptively. These thresholds are fixed before outcomes are scored. Source restrictions, observational release selection and the encoder's exposure to earlier random logs must remain explicit.

### History-only state construction implementation

`configs/human-history-state-v1.json` fixes a 256-state GRU-history quantizer and 64 initial predictive blocks. It samples at most 100,000 standard-policy training cases uniformly without replacement using seed 20261015. All canonical random case IDs are excluded from this sample before reading encoder inputs. The encoder itself was trained on the original mixed training data, which remains an explicit limitation. Mean/scale and MiniBatchKMeans use this standard-only sample. Initial blocks are stable equal-parent-count bins ordered by sampled standard-exposure teacher probability; these means are initialization features, not final response calibration. Empty sampled states use the global sampled teacher mean for initialization. History features are `encode(tokens)` only; current scenario, item and duration enter the teacher initialization prediction but never the history representation.

This design is separate from the frozen D6 post-exposure representation and does not revise its negative compression results. Cloud smoke must pass before constructing history-state artifacts. Fit provenance binds the completed GRU checkpoint, training NPZ, audited random case IDs, configuration and implementation. The current source has no final-test scoring path. All resulting history states, discoveries and future group-response estimates remain private on Modal; reports export only aggregate support, hashes and partition metadata. Calibration/refinement configuration, power and final statistical freeze remain open.

`response_refinement.py` now implements discovery-witness midpoint splitting, a random refinement control that preserves the initial classes and exactly matches the target state count, and separate-estimation point emissions. Parent Bernoulli estimates use a Jeffreys pseudocount; zero-support cells use the environment-wide Jeffreys mean. Reduced emissions mix parent emissions using weights equal to cell count plus one. These are explicit point-model choices. Their displayed parent/quotient discrepancy is numerical description, not an exact arithmetic certificate or human confidence bound. Witness splitting is a heuristic, not a significance test. Unsupported contexts and unknown durations must not be promoted to inferential evidence. These helpers are tested but no human refinement comparison has run.
