For an origin latent state s, emit y with probability E(s,y), then transition to s' with T(s,y,s'). Forward filtering sums over every unobserved future latent state. At each emission, normalize the forward vector and accumulate minus log of its mass. No future content, future encoder state assignment, or observed transition label is supplied as an input. A zero-probability sequence receives infinite NLL; no silent clipping is allowed. For H observations there are H emissions and H-1 latent transitions.
