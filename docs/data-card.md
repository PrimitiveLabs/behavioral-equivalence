# Human-data card

Dataset: KuaiRand, Gao et al. (2022), DOI 10.1145/3511808.3557624. Use the official distribution and maintain its attribution. This release contains processing code, aggregate research outputs and rights records, not raw human interaction rows.

The final study uses the KuaiRand-27K deposit identified in data/license-register.json. The exact archive is 9,892,191,178 bytes, MD5 3e3c799a24e2d23a4d2c757fbf9adf59. Matching deposit metadata declares CC-BY-4.0; completed inspection found no embedded license in that archive. The KuaiRand-1K pilot separately contains a CC-BY-SA-4.0 license. Do not transfer the pilot archive's embedded terms to the full archive. Both declared licenses permit commercial use subject to their terms. Project policy restricts the current work to private attributed research; raw redistribution, weights release and product integration are excluded pending artifact-specific review. These are project release boundaries, not claims that CC-BY prohibits commercial research.

Preparation contains approximately 309.1 million rows and 26,285 users after pilot exclusion. Do not describe this as using all 322 million interactions in the original release. Immutable physical source IDs, timestamp bundles, representatives, exclusions and calendar roles have independent audit records. The observation policy and confirmatory calendar are in spec/data-observation-policy.md and spec/confirmatory-calendar.md.

Known-user evaluation forecasts later observations after chronological training, validation and calibration. Unseen-user evaluation uses disjoint user groups and the same calendar policy. Input histories contain only feedback allowed by the stated availability/embargo rule. Training vocabulary and quantization use training data; final tolerances and statistical decisions use the internal analysis lock. Actual feedback observation timestamps are not supplied by the dataset, so embargo sensitivity does not establish perfect reconstruction of real production availability.

Primary labels are recorded clicks/non-clicks. Supplementary labels are explicit like, follow, comment, forward and hate feedback, plus absolute watch-time categories [0,1000), [1000,5000), [5000,30000), [30000,infinity) milliseconds. Invalid or missing supplementary labels remain unknown and are excluded endpoint-wise. A non-click is not dislike. Unavailable future horizons are censored, not intentional abandonment. Purchase and task-completion labels are unavailable.

Repeated observations are clustered by original user. Shared calendar shocks, exposure-policy effects, user selection, learning on known users and latent-state misspecification remain limitations. Randomly exposed content supports limited recommendation-response analyses, not arbitrary software-interface interventions or a population causal-effect guarantee. No raw user identifiers or individual forecasts are included in public artifacts.

The final-analysis freeze, complete source audits and result hashes document the implemented split and estimand. Reproduction of private human experiments requires obtaining the official data under its applicable terms and provisioning the same private Modal layout. Synthetic reproduction requires no human data.
