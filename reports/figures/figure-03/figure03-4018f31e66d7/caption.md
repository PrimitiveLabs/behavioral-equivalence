The 1,200-world frozen matrix crosses five mechanisms, 32/128/512 states, four perturbations and twenty seeds. Every panel contains all 26,400 quotient variants, including uncertified clustering and random controls. The horizontal coordinate displays 1-(1-epsilon)^H using the exact-normalized one-step radius recorded by the integer auditor, rounded for plotting. The directed-integer certificates remain the numerical evidence; displayed radii and dots do not replace their exact arithmetic. The certificates use actual serialized radii. These implementation observations do not invalidate their bounds, but they prevent interpreting every requested epsilon as an exact achieved tolerance.
