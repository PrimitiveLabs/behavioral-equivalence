## Exact-normalized one-step model certificates

Each fixed scenario/duration context defines a chain with pending latent states and two absorbing observable labels, positive and negative. Parent states map to each candidate's latent classes, and outcome states remain separate. The abstract initial law is the pushforward of the uniform 32-state parent law. The existing integer engine checks these supplied point kernels; it does not assume the quotient uses uniform emission weights.

Certificate generation and replay cover all 20 candidates and 75 fixed contexts per fitted seed. The scope is one-step positive/negative events for exact binary-rational row weights normalized within each row. This scope does not imply human calibration, arbitrary-context causal transfer or human trajectory preservation. Raw floating predictor emissions and normalized row semantics can differ at serialization precision; applying a certificate to a different numeric predictor requires accounting for that difference.

The engine's general mathematical assumptions and proof-review limitations remain unchanged. Arithmetic replay with the existing verifier is not independent theorem review or an additional independent PRISM experiment. Worst-case state/context radii can be wide even where case-weighted predictions eventually prove useful; both facts must be reported without replacing one estimand with another.

All 7,500 certificates have now been generated and replayed: 20 candidates times 75 contexts times five fitted seeds. Primary worst-case one-step state/context discrepancies are 0.392857, 0.407143, 0.407143, 0.392857 and 0.407143. These are wide, about 39 to 41 percentage points. The certificates therefore do not establish tight uniform prediction preservation for these primary point models. They also do not determine held-out average human loss, which remains unmeasured. No source of these worst cases is attributed without checking its witness and support.

The initial history classes are uncertified predictive clusters. Splitting them does not demonstrate invalidation of a previously sound neutral-environment bisimulation certificate. That mathematical witness-recovery result belongs to the separately controlled synthetic experiment.

The reported 0.393-0.407 maxima range over all75model contexts, including the unknown-duration descriptive group. The primary future-response cohort uses known-duration contexts. Maxima for that restricted context family and their witness support have not yet been separately summarized; do not substitute the all-context maximum for an unevaluated restricted-family result.
