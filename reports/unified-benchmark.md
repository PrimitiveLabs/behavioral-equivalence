## Verification and numeric boundaries

Supplementary replay checks 340 files, 7,200 proper-loss cells, all reported class/top calibration cells and 1,860 contrasts. It is not external coverage of every large uncertainty world. The full mathematical argument is analytical with separately checked Lean components; external peer review remains unperformed.

## Failure analysis and release boundaries

Observed failures include failed primary compression, wide/vacuous intervals, unsupported within-state strata, unfavorable human response intervals, identical targeted/control forecasts, negative active-search comparisons, tiny serialized numerical radii and retained environment/setup failures. They are scientific or engineering findings with their original evidence, not excluded runs.

Actual feedback-arrival timestamps, arbitrary interface response data, population treatment effects, unknown human kernel membership and general human Markov sufficiency remain unavailable or unestablished. Raw interactions, trained weights and individual predictions stay private. Code, synthetic specifications, aggregate results, proofs, figure data and permitted evaluation materials form the release. Data rights and model cards retain their separate terms and scopes.
