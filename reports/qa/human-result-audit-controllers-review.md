# Independent human result audit controller review

Read audit_final_human.py, audit_supplementary.py, audit_trajectories.py and
audit_randomized_cases.py in full. No executable source changed or cloud function
was invoked. This scoped review is not whole-release acceptance.

Final human audit authenticates scored reports, embedded source identities and
saved output bytes. It reconstructs direct-horizon and response forecasts from
retained factorized arrays, verifies ordered physical case IDs and independently
combines sorted-user proper losses. Primary user-bootstrap contrasts are replayed
with their stored replication counts and seeds. It intentionally excludes
secondary subgroup calibration and fresh neural forward passes. A completed
partial five-job audit is not full-program evidence; the selected later audit
covers all fifty jobs, and its report hashes exactly match current frozen job
progress and local scored reports.

Supplementary audit requires the fixed scored source, authenticates calibration
and forecast arrays, reconstructs every endpoint/comparator inventory, and checks
proper losses, categorical and top calibration bins, private user statistics and
paired bootstrap comparisons. All ten retained fit reports bind to the exact
local scored report hashes and summary counts. The current calibration helper
has a later finite-value correction, separately preserved and reviewed; historical
successful cloud runs must not be represented as validating that new source.
Uniform-envelope interval diagnostics are outside this independent audit.

Trajectory audit requires exactly both protocols and five seeds, authenticates
parent and quotient arrays, checks shared sampled IDs across fits and recomputes
joint likelihoods using backward rather than forward recursion. Infinite support
failures remain explicit, and finite per-user losses and bootstrap intervals use
observed-horizon cohorts. The audit does not independently regenerate outcome-
blind sampled trajectories or prove that the finite-state model describes humans.
Its retained ten-fit matrix binds to current local reports and records 450 metric
cells and 400 bootstrap contrasts. Legacy numeric comparisons assume the trusted
finite scored-report domain; they are not a general adversarial JSON validator.

Randomized cohort audit uses a separate PyArrow full-source reconstruction,
checks original exposure rows and full histories, and independently calculates
intrinsic duration availability, duration thresholds and all 75 environment
support cells. The complete retained 32-bucket audit binds to the exact prepared
cohort report and agrees with its role/protocol support totals. Cached successful
audit reports are trusted historical records, not live proof that private bytes
still match. No private arrays were downloaded or rehashed during this review.

Local evidence reconciliation is retained in
reports/preflight/human-result-audit-controller-reconciliation.json. It verifies
all fifty primary, ten supplementary and ten trajectory scored-report identities,
finite retained numerical discrepancies and the complete randomized audit scope.
It does not rerun private-data loss calculations or current cloud source.

Supplementary and trajectory launchers have lifetime locks and exclusive attempt
markers; the older final-human and randomized launchers do not have the same
retry/concurrency protections. None was invoked. Provider failures, transport,
concurrent writes and fresh image builds were not exercised. Existing focused
library/tool tests remain separately scoped. No tests were rerun for report-only
changes. Strict final environment validation, remaining source review and final
release acceptance stay open.
