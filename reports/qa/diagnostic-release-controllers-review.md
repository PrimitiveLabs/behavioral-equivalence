supplementary forecasts and original case/context sidecars, align physical IDs
and users, and prohibit users crossing buckets. They summarize within-state
history, missing-context and scenario cells using equal-user arithmetic and
minimum support. This is explicitly post-hoc descriptive heterogeneity, not
Markov sufficiency, causal identification, adjusted discovery or significance.
The current exact integer identity helper correction is separately documented;
retained historical execution uses the original helper.

coarsening maps, then counts only calibration-role exposure/state/environment
support with a stable discovery/estimation user split. No response targets are
read. Total cases/users must match the independently audited randomized cohort.
Outcome-free support does not establish useful prediction or behavioral
similarity, and sparse/unknown-duration exclusions remain explicit.

Synthetic diversity analysis authenticates the terminal campaign and generating
source, requires all frozen worlds, verifies per-world specification and bytes,
and replays all 48 span/context certificates before paired world aggregation.
Cached world audits bind report/model hashes and replay counts. The resulting
400-world comparisons are synthetic evidence, not actual human intervention
responses or conversion lift. Shared-world pairing and negative controls remain
part of the frozen campaign.

absolute/traversal/non-file tar members, verifies manifest hashes, builds one
wheel and tests its installed import with source-path injection disabled. It
requires warnings-as-errors and zero test skips, then executes the small scientific
rerun with pinned PRISM. Dependencies, checker source, wheel and source bundle
identities are recorded. It does not rerun the full human or 1200-world benchmark
or supply independent theorem review. The launcher has a lifetime lock, exclusive
attempt marker, preserved handle and source/checker-specific reservation.
