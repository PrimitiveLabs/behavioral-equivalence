The primary one-step, horizon and response evaluators require the joint analysis
freeze before reading final test rows. Source/config hashes and bound upstream
artifacts prevent silently applying revised helpers to the historical freeze.
The internal pretest lock is not an external preregistration, and previously known
pilot/validation failures remain disclosed. The existing original-source restoration
instructions are necessary for historical reproduction with corrected helpers.

One-step scoring authenticates neural fits, training-only preprocessing, quantizers,
calibrated parents, nested abstractions and interval arrays. Every method uses
identical physical case/user supports. Fixed zero/24/48-hour feedback sensitivities
reuse 24-hour-trained models and are labeled as sensitivity analyses. Identity-
sized primary models must reproduce the normalized parent exactly; zero loss
difference does not satisfy the separate compression requirement. Pooled interval
diagnostics compare cohort quantities and are not human probability coverage proofs.

Horizon scoring authenticates original test trajectories, parent/quotient dynamics
and frozen direct heads. Forecast features use origin context only. Missing future
targets are masked per event, retaining the conditional observed-horizon estimand.
Frozen-origin IID comparators are explicitly distinct from direct heads and joint
sequence prediction. Floating event tables clipped to [0,1] are screening forecasts,
not formal certificates; proper-loss clipping is consistently disclosed. Lossless
factorized archives permit independent numerical loss replay.

Response evaluation requires the outcome-free exact case/user/ID eligibility
contract. All six duration holdouts are checked. For each original exposure,
three applicable held-out forecast losses are averaged before aggregation within
original users. Neither fold copies nor shared fitted seeds are independent human
samples. Query restrictions and training priors support only the specified recorded
exposure study, not arbitrary software changes or causal effects. Strong baseline
comparisons and unsupported cohorts remain visible.

Supplementary evaluation requires a separately frozen ten-fit/six-endpoint design,
authenticates train-selected heads, calibration tables, labels and physical case
alignment, and fits baseline tables exclusively on fitting-cohort training rows.
All comparators retain identical observed-endpoint user/case supports. Endpoint
contrasts are explicitly descriptive, not a rescue of locked primary hypotheses.
Uniform abstract envelopes and empirical cohort compatibility diagnostics do not
establish human confidence coverage; the report sets that claim to false.
